Research interests

Working paper

Market Your Share! Marketing Capabilities, Investor Relations and Firm Performance

Using a panel of U.S. firms (2015–2020), we examine how Analyst & Investor Relationships (AIR) complement firms’ marketing capabilities (MC) to create firm value (Tobin’s q), including effects of communicating MC in earnings calls.
