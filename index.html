Working papers

Talk the Walk: Predicting Pricing Power Using Top Management Team’s Language

With Niels Holtrop, Joost M.E. Pennings, Thomas Post, and Hauke Wetzel

Pricing power is a fundamental driver of long-term value, yet accounting measures like the price–cost margin are backward-looking and cost-contaminated. Using dependency parsing on earnings calls, we build a continuous, firm-level measure of communicated pricing power, decomposed into realized and expected signals. Across 55,664 firm-quarters (3,138 U.S. firms, 2018–2024), the realized component aligns with accounting proxies while the expected component anticipates future price–cost margins — managerial language moves before the accounting record.

Market Your Share! How Marketing Capability and its Communication Drive Firm Value through Analyst & Investor Relationships

With Niels Holtrop, Joost M.E. Pennings, and Thomas Post

Using survey-based Analyst & Investor Relationships (AIR) across 1,159 U.S. firms (2015–2020), we show AIR contributes positively to Total q. Marketing capabilities strengthen AIR, and communicating MC in earnings calls amplifies this link. Moderation analyses reveal context-dependent pathways through analyst coverage and institutional ownership.
