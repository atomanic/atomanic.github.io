Market Your Share! How Marketing Capability and its Communication Shape Analyst & Investor Relationships

André Tomano (working paper)

We study how analyst & investor relationships (AIR) complement firms’ marketing capabilities (MC) to create firm value (Total q). Using a panel of U.S. firms (2015–2020) and survey-based AIR, we show positive value contributions and communication effects via earnings calls.
